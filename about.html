About Airbnb Clone

Airbnb Clone is a project designed to replicate the look and feel of the Airbnb website. It showcases listings of unique places to stay around the world, providing users with a seamless experience to find their next stay.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Airbnb Clone was created as a learning project to demonstrate web development skills including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Our Mission

To provide a user-friendly platform that connects travelers with unique accommodations worldwide.
